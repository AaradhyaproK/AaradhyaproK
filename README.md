## 👨‍💻 Professional Summary & Bio
I am **Aaradhya Pathak**, a **Full-Stack Software Engineer**, **Product Architect**, and **Founder** at **[Snab Innovation](https://www.snab.co.in)**. I specialize in building high-throughput consumer SaaS, privacy-centric WebAssembly (Wasm) engines, and multimodal Generative AI platforms using **Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node.js, and Java (DSA)**.

- 🏢 **Snab Innovation:** Lead software architecture and venture engineering across a portfolio of high-growth digital products at [snab.co.in](https://www.snab.co.in).
- ⚡ **FileZenith:** Architected a 100% private, client-side document utility suite with 70+ WASM tools, 211 production pages, and **50,000+ monthly Google search impressions** at [filezenith.com](https://www.filezenith.com).
- 💳 **FeeKit:** Engineered the automated recurring fee collection, instant tax receipt generator, and invoice reconciliation platform at [usefeekit.com](https://www.usefeekit.com).
- 🎯 **InterviewXpert:** Built an AI-driven interview simulation engine delivering real-time candidate assessment, question generation, and speech analysis at [interviewxpert.in](https://www.interviewxpert.in).
- 🏆 **Startup Arena 2.0:** Secured **2nd Prize** and selected for the Zonal Round at **IIT Bombay**.
- 🤖 **GenAI Innovation:** Awarded **Top 10** at GenAI Hackathon for building **AI Base**.
- 📈 **Leadership:** Led **IEDC (4,000+ members)** and secured **All India Rank 64**.
